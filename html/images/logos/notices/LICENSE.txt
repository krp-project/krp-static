-------------------------------

Copyright Austrian Science Fund (FWF).

All FWF-funded researchers are asked to name the FWF as the funding institution prominently
in all project-related documents and documents for dissemination/science communications, and
to include the FWF logo. Information on project funding should be worded in a neutral and
appropriate manner. (See https://www.fwf.ac.at/en/news/press/logos.)

The FWF logo also represents a seal of quality for excellent basic research, so we recommend
ensuring that it is reproduced in a legible size with sufficient distance to other logos.

Copyright University of Vienna.

The use of the logos of the University of Vienna requires the express consent of the
University of Vienna. The logos may only be used by university employees for work-related
purposes. The logos must not be used by or transferred to third parties, for example to
cooperation partners, without the University’s prior consent.
(See https://www.univie.ac.at/en/about-us/organisation-and-structure/corporate-communications/downloads/.)
